Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026.

Who uses this app

Gemini Health Bridge is a personal tool used only by its developer, with his own Google account.

What it reads

With the account owner's permission, it reads Google Health data through Google's read-only scopes: activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements, and sleep. It never writes to or changes that data.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to produce the account owner's own summaries. It is stored on the owner's own computer and in his own Google account. It is not sold, shared, transferred to anyone else, or used for advertising, and the app has no analytics or tracking.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Gemini Health Bridge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in Google Account settings, under Security, then Your connections to third-party apps and services.
